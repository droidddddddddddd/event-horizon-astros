Privacy

This site has no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no backend. Studio images and uploaded photos are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded; exported files carry no camera metadata because they are re-drawn on a canvas. Mission progress and the optional crew callsign are stored on your device only (localStorage / sessionStorage).

The rank checker sends a public wallet address you paste to public Solana RPC endpoints (read-only) and, once a token exists, queries DexScreener's public API. Those providers see your IP address under their own policies. The optional Phantom connection exposes your public address to this page; it is not transmitted or stored, and no signature or transaction is requested.

Hosting providers may keep standard access logs. External links are governed by their own policies. Operator identity, contact and jurisdiction are not yet published; they must be added before any sale.
