    FAQ

    Is anything live?

    No. This is a prelaunch concept site. There is no live mint, no sale and no token on sale through this site. Mainnet payments are disabled in code and configuration.

    What are Astros?

    Planned collectible astronaut avatars on Solana: 1,000 Astros in five tiers (550 Common, 250 Uncommon, 120 Rare, 60 Epic, 20 Legendary, including five one-of-one pieces). Art is concept art until final approval.

    How would the mint work?

    Proposed: 200 early crew at 0.025 SOL, 780 public at 0.05 SOL, and 20 disclosed community reserve. Public limit two per wallet. Proposed 2.5% secondary royalty, subject to marketplace support. Network fees are extra. Dates and final terms are not confirmed.

    What about the $ASTRO token?

    Planned Pump.fun launch targeting 1 billion supply, simultaneous with the NFT window. Not launched, no contract, no date. Holding or buying anything is not required to mint. No returns are promised. Meme tokens can go to zero.

    Are the studio and rank checker safe?

    The studio runs fully in your browser; images are never uploaded. The rank checker reads a pasted public address through read-only RPC calls. We never ask for a seed phrase or private key and never will. Perks are cosmetic.

    Do fan creations or missions earn rewards?

    No. Fan creations and mission progress confer no ownership and no financial rewards.
